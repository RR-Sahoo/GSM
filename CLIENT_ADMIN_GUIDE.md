# GSM Solar Light — Website Preview & Staff CMS User Guide

Welcome to the preview website for **GSM Solar Light** (*Mathura, Uttar Pradesh*).
This guide explains how to review the website, how your team can use the **Staff CMS (`/admin`)**, and what final details we need from you before launching on your official domain.

---

## 1. Pages to Review

Please check each page on both **mobile** and **desktop**:

1. **Home** (`/`) — Hero banner, capability strip (*Up to 1 MW Solar Project Capability*, *Multiple 10 kW Rooftop Installations*), Solar Solutions, 6 Product Categories, Brands & Partnerships, Project Showcase, FAQs, and Call/WhatsApp CTA.
2. **About Us** (`/about`) — Company overview, Vision, Mission, business verticals, and Mathura location.
3. **Solar Solutions** (`/solutions`) — Residential, Commercial, Industrial, and Rooftop Solar sections with targeted enquiry buttons.
4. **Products** (`/products`) — Filterable catalogue across all 6 categories with product detail pages and prefilled WhatsApp enquiry buttons.
5. **Our Brands & Distribution Partnerships** (`/brands`) — Top-level page separating **Mtek Power** & **Getsun Power** (*Current Distributors*) from **UTL** (*Previous Distribution Partnership*).
6. **Our Projects** (`/projects`) — Filterable gallery (*Residential / Commercial / Industrial*). Current entries are marked as **Sample Slots** until your approved installation photos are uploaded.
7. **Contact Us** (`/contact`) — Lead-routed enquiry form (Customer Type, Requirement, Capacity, City, Consent), click-to-call, WhatsApp button, and Mathura map link.

## 2. How to Use the Staff CMS (`/admin`)

1. Open `<your-website-url>/admin`
2. Sign in with the temporary preview credentials shared separately with you *(we will change the password before final launch)*.

> **Preview Note:** While hosted on the temporary Vercel preview link, any edits or test enquiries submitted in `/admin` are for demonstration only and may reset automatically. Before final launch on your live domain, we will connect permanent storage so all products, uploads, and leads are saved permanently.

### Inside `/admin`, you have 5 tabs:

1. **Enquiries**
   - View every enquiry submitted through the website forms, including **Customer Type**, **Requirement**, **Capacity**, **City**, **Source Page**, and **Product Name**.
   - Update lead status between `New`, `Contacted`, and `Closed`.
2. **Products**
   - Click **+ Add Product** or **Edit** on any existing product.
   - Choose the **Category**, optional **Distribution Brand** (`Mtek Power` or `Getsun Power`), add descriptions, and add **Specification rows** (e.g., Model, Capacity, Voltage).
   - Add an image URL or upload a product photo / PDF datasheet (in final permanent hosting), and toggle **Active** or **Featured**.
3. **Brands**
   - Update summaries, descriptions, notes, and approved logos for **Mtek Power**, **Getsun Power**, and **UTL**.
   - Keep **UTL** set to `Previous Distribution Partnership` unless your status changes.
4. **Projects**
   - Click **+ Add Project** or **Edit** a sample slot to add real installation titles, capacities, city names, descriptions, and customer-approved photos.
   - Uncheck **Mark as Sample Slot (Placeholder)** once real photos are added.
5. **Settings & Confirmations**
   - Update your official **Phone**, **WhatsApp Number**, **Email**, **Complete Mathura Address**, **Google Maps Link**, and optional **Google Analytics 4 ID**.
   - Control these 4 confirmation switches:
     - **Enable Ground-Mounted Solar** *(currently OFF — enable only if you offer this service)*
     - **Enable Project Installation & Execution** *(currently OFF — enable once workflow steps are approved)*
     - **Use "Get a Free Consultation" wording** *(currently OFF — enable only if consultations are free)*
     - **Publish Product Prices publicly** *(currently OFF — keeps products in "Request Price / Enquire" mode)*
